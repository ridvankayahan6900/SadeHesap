## Full description (≤4000 characters)
Sade Hesap is a clean, fast calculator for your everyday calculations. It requests no permissions, never connects to the internet, and collects no data.

**Features**
- Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentage
- Correct order-of-operations calculation (multiplication and division are applied first)
- Precise decimal calculation, with trailing zeros trimmed automatically
- Sign toggle (±) and backspace (⌫)
- History of your last 20 calculations; tap any entry to reuse its result
- Long-press a result to copy it to the clipboard in one tap
- Dynamic color support on Android 12+, automatic light/dark theme
- Full support for both portrait and landscape orientation
- Accessibility: a content description on every key (screen reader support), large touch targets

**Privacy first**
Sade Hesap does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app requests no permissions at all (including internet access), shows no ads, and includes no third-party SDKs. Your calculation history is kept in memory only while the app is open and is never saved anywhere.

The app's source code is open.
